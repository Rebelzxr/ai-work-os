# Deliverable production — copy-paste edition

## What to give it

- The approved scope and first production brief.
- Supplied source files or notes, with labels and dates where useful.
- Target format, included work, exclusions and acceptance criteria.
- Any known correction limit, permission note or unresolved question.

## The prompt

```text
Create the scoped deliverable from the approved brief and supplied sources.

Begin with a short production header listing objective, deliverables, exclusions, source list, target format and acceptance criteria. Check whether each criterion can be met from the supplied sources. Create the artifact in the workspace, tie every factual claim to a source or mark it as owner input, and keep an unresolved list for missing, conflicting or unclear inputs.

Return the artifact or its workspace path, a production note, changed files, source list, unresolved items, and an observation for every acceptance criterion. Keep the result marked draft or needs review. Do not invent a source, testimonial, review, result, permission or client decision, and do not call it final before a separate quality gate. Begin with “Practice, not for use” for invented examples.
```

## What you get back

- The local artifact or a path to it.
- A production note with scope and sources.
- An unresolved list.
- Criterion-by-criterion observations for the separate quality gate.

## Check before you use it

Open the artifact, match each claim to a supplied source, check the unresolved list, and confirm that no extra request has become part of the work. A separate reviewer must decide the gate result.
