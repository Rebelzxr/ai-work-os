# Discovery call planner — copy-paste edition

## What to give it

- A checked business brief with offer, fit rules, scope limits and capacity.
- A prospect label, public facts and source dates.
- The purpose and planned length of the conversation.
- Any questions already on the owner's list.

## The prompt

```text
Prepare a one-page discovery-call plan from the supplied business brief and prospect facts.

State one decision the conversation should inform. Give a simple timebox, then write exactly eight open questions about the prospect's past behaviour, what was tried, the cost or effect of the problem, the desired result, fit and decision process. Add evidence to listen for, disqualifiers from the brief, notes fields, and three possible next steps: scope a proposal, request a missing input, or close as not a fit.

Use only supplied facts and label anything unknown. Do not invent a budget, authority, deadline, problem, outcome or booking. Do not present the conversation as completed. Start with “Practice, not for use” when the example is invented.
```

## What you get back

- A call goal and timebox.
- Eight questions grounded in past behaviour.
- Fit signals, disqualifiers, notes fields and three next-step choices.
- An unverified-facts list.

## Check before you use it

Make sure each question helps the stated decision, each disqualifier comes from the brief, and no unknown has been written as a fact. The owner checks the plan before the conversation.
