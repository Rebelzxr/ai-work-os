# Scope proposal

This job makes the work and its boundaries visible before an owner decides whether to offer it. It is not a price guesser and it is not a contract.

## Start safely

Use an invented Malaysian business, invented discovery notes and invented rate rules on the first run. Label the result **practice, not for use**. Do not ask for private client messages or identifying details.

For later work, the owner supplies a checked brief, cleaned discovery notes, the requested outcome, and the exact rate rules that may be used. If the rate rules are absent, stop the price section rather than filling it from memory.

Clean discovery notes locally first. Remove names, identity numbers, phone numbers, bank details, addresses and raw private messages; use a prospect label and keep the original outside the AI workspace. Never ask the AI to clean an unclean record after upload.

## Required inputs

- The checked business brief.
- A cleaned discovery record with the prospect labelled, not named.
- The requested outcome and known constraints.
- Rate rules with units, inclusions, exclusions, minimums, discounts and payment timing, if approved by the owner.
- Any known deadline, source-material dependency and correction limit.

## Steps

1. State the objective in one sentence and separate it from the requested method.
2. List deliverables with a clear form, quantity and owner-side input needed for each.
3. List exclusions. Put every out-of-scope request in **not included**, **needs a change decision** or **needs more information**; do not quietly absorb it.
4. Set milestones and dependencies using only supplied dates or label dates as undecided.
5. Write acceptance criteria that a person can check, plus the revision or correction limit.
6. Build the price only from the supplied rate rules. Show each quantity, unit rate, rule, arithmetic and subtotal. If a rule or quantity is missing, show `price pending owner rule` and list the exact question.
7. Add the supplied payment schedule. If timing, deposit, tax, currency or late terms are missing, flag them; never invent a term.
8. Keep testimonials, reviews, case results and guarantees out unless the owner supplies a dated, permission-checked source and explicitly allows the claim.

## Output

Return a review draft with: objective, deliverables, exclusions, milestones, dependencies, acceptance criteria, revision limit, open questions, rate-rule calculation, payment schedule, assumptions and an owner-check list. Mark the draft **not final** until every missing term is resolved.

## Failure modes

- Never make up a price, discount, tax, currency, deposit, deadline or term.
- Never use a rate rule for a different unit or quietly round a quantity.
- Never promise a result or add an invented testimonial.
- Never hide an out-of-scope request inside a deliverable.
- Never call the proposal accepted or delivered without a supplied owner record.

## Human approval

The owner checks scope, rate rules, calculations, exclusions, acceptance criteria and terms. A person approves the exact proposal and any commercial action; this skill only writes a local review draft.
