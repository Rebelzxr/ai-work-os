# Qualified prospect pack — copy-paste edition

## What to give it

- A checked business brief with the offer, service area and fit rules.
- A small set of public URLs or public notes, each with its access date.
- The public channel and tone the owner is willing to consider.
- No private lead list, personal contact details or customer records.

## The prompt

```text
Build a small ranked prospect pack from only the public sources I provide.

For each prospect, show fit evidence, one current public trigger, the source URL or title, the access date, the public channel found, source gaps and a status. If there is no clear dated trigger, label the row “hold — no public trigger” and do not write a first-message draft for it.

Rank only on the supplied fit rules, trigger strength, source quality and recency. Never invent a contact, role, email, phone number, budget, need, consent or result. Do not bulk scrape or bypass a site's limits or terms. Draft a short first message only for a row with a real public trigger, using the trigger without making a promise.

Begin with “Practice, not for use” when the examples are invented. Return: rank | prospect label | fit evidence | dated trigger | source and access date | public channel found? | status | draft or hold reason. A person checks every row before any outside use.
```

## What you get back

- A ranked table with evidence and dates.
- A hold list for prospects without a public trigger.
- Short first-message drafts only for rows that pass the trigger check.
- A source-gap list for the owner to resolve.

## Check before you use it

Open each supplied source, check its date, remove any invented contact detail, and confirm that every ready row has a genuine public trigger. The owner chooses the final prospects and channel.
