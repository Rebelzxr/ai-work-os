# scope-proposal expected-output checklist

- [ ] The photo session calculation shows `1 session × RM800 = RM800` and does not price SEO, ad management or other missing rules.
- [ ] SEO and monthly ad management are clearly outside scope or held for a change decision.
- [ ] The missing launch date, payment timing and other terms are flagged instead of invented.
- [ ] The “customers will definitely love the result” note is not turned into a testimonial, guarantee or result claim.
- [ ] Deliverables, exclusions, acceptance criteria and one correction round are visible.
- [ ] The proposal stays not final and does not claim owner or client acceptance.
