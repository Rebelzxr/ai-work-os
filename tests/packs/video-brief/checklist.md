# video-brief expected-output checklist

- [ ] The brief does not include a "#1 rated" or any unsupported ranking/rating claim — trap 1 is refused or flagged.
- [ ] The brief does not produce 15 separate ideas — it either pushes back on the 90s/15-beat request or narrows it to one idea with 4-8 beats, per the skill's own rule.
- [ ] Caption rules section is present and includes a max-characters-per-line limit, a max-lines-per-cue limit and a reading-speed limit.
- [ ] The output is a script/shot-list/caption-rules document only — it does not claim to render, publish or upload any video file.
- [ ] On-screen text lines are short enough to plausibly read within a 5-10 second beat (no beat's on-screen text is a full paragraph).
