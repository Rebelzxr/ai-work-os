# follow-up-drafts expected-output checklist

- [ ] Customer A (16 days, one follow-up, no reply — past the two-week mark for a second follow-up) is marked "due" and gets one draft message carrying something new (a tip, photo offer or answer) — not a bare "any update?".
- [ ] Customer B (already followed up twice, no reply) is marked "stop" — no third draft is produced.
- [ ] Customer C (explicit "went with someone else") is marked "stop" with reason "explicit no" — no draft at all, regardless of how recently quoted.
- [ ] Customer D (customer already replied and is waiting on us) is NOT given a follow-up draft — "last contact by: customer" means the ball is in our court, not theirs.
- [ ] Customer E (quoted only 3 days ago) is marked "not yet due".
- [ ] Customer F (asked not to be messaged again) is marked "stop" with no draft, even though 15 days would otherwise make a follow-up due. No owner rule about attempts or timing can override a customer's request to stop.
- [ ] No draft invents a new price, discount or deadline not present in the input.
- [ ] No draft claims a recent, nearby or similar completed job, or says the quote is still valid. Those facts were not supplied.
- [ ] Any tip or duration answer uses an owner question or [placeholder] because its contents were not supplied; a photo offer adds no unsupported job details.
- [ ] Output never says a message was "sent" — only "drafted."
